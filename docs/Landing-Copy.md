Every text element on the landing page is specced here. Structure comes from Landing-Page-Spec.md; this doc fills in the words.

### 1.1 Status of this doc

**Every copy line is a STARTER DRAFT** — written in the voice implied by what Mac has shared, but not yet approved by Mac. Review workflow:

2. Marks each line as one of: KEEP / TWEAK / REPLACE
3. TWEAK lines get inline edits

5. Once every line is KEEP or has an approved edit, this doc goes to final

**Special attention on:** Founder story (§4.3), contrarian opinion (§4.4), and any line that mentions a specific channel niche Mac tried — these can only be his to write authentically. Placeholders provided to react to, not to ship.

### 1.2 Voice guidelines (from what Mac has shared)

- **Direct, no fluff.** No "revolutionize your workflow" or "unlock your potential."
- **Honest about failure.** The tool exists because Mac himself got stuck. Don't hide that.
- **Not corporate.** Sentences can be incomplete. Contractions welcome. Occasional casual line.
- **Warm but not saccharine.** No "we love our creators!" energy. Respect the reader's intelligence.
- **Position: Research + Execution.** Every section reinforces that competitors stop at niche finding; YTNiches continues.
- **Never dunk on competitors by name in the main copy.** Save that for VS pages, and even there stay respectful.

### 1.3 Sentence-level rules

- Prefer short sentences. If a sentence runs past 20 words, break it up.
- Active voice always. "YTNiches finds outliers" not "outliers are found by YTNiches."
- Concrete verbs. "Ship" beats "deliver." "Find" beats "discover." "Steal" beats "learn from."
- One idea per sentence. Don't stack clauses.
- Numbers > adjectives. "90 days of content" beats "lots of content."

### 1.4 Words to use

Faceless creator, niche, outlier, competitor, prompt, script, thumbnail, channel, videos, workspace, calendar, spy, extract, ship, stranded, stuck, find, steal, build.

### 1.5 Words to avoid

Revolutionize, unlock, empower, seamless, cutting-edge, best-in-class, next-gen, disrupt, transform, journey, ecosystem, holistic, synergy, delight.

### 1.6 Formatting conventions in this doc

- `[STARTER DRAFT]` prefix on any line Mac needs to review
- `[NEEDS MAC INPUT]` prefix on any placeholder that requires personal detail Claude cannot invent
- `[OPTION A / OPTION B / OPTION C]` = multiple starter drafts for the same slot; Mac picks one
- `[COPY]` in Landing-Page-Spec.md maps to a section here

### 5.5 Copy formulas (for future writing)

Use these patterns when adding new copy — keeps voice consistent.

**Headline formula:** `[Concrete action or observation]. [Contrast or consequence].`

- ✅ "Every tool helps you find a niche. Then leaves you stranded."
- ✅ "Find niches. Steal from outliers. Ship 90 days of content."
- ❌ "Revolutionize your YouTube workflow with AI-powered insights."

**Subhead formula:** Restate the promise + name the mechanism.

- ✅ "Most AI writing tools guess. YTNiches feeds AI the actual winning video's structure."
- ❌ "Powerful AI features for modern content creators."

**Body paragraph formula:** Scenario → pain → resolution. Max 3 sentences.

**CTA formula:** Verb + benefit + friction-buster.

- ✅ "Sign up free — no card"
- ✅ "Start free trial — 14 days"
- ❌ "Get started today!"
