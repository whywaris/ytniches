We want you to be happy paying for YTNiches. If it isn't working for you, here's what we refund.

## Money-back guarantee

Your first purchase comes with a <Fact k="refunds.guaranteeDays" />-day money-back guarantee. That covers monthly and yearly plans.

## After the guarantee

- **Monthly plans:** no refunds after the first <Fact k="refunds.guaranteeDays" unit="day" />.
- **Yearly plans:** cancel within the first <Fact k="refunds.annualWindowDays" unit="day" /> and we refund the unused whole months.

## Exceptions

We look at these case by case:

- A billing error, like a double charge or the wrong plan.
- An extended service outage.
- A documented account compromise.

## How to ask

Email <SupportLink /> from the email on your account and tell us what happened. We'll reply with a decision.

Our payment processor, acting as Merchant of Record, sends approved refunds to your original payment method. How long it takes to reach you depends on your bank.

## Cancelling instead

You don't need a refund to stop paying. Cancel from **Settings → Billing** and you won't be charged again. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. See the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).
