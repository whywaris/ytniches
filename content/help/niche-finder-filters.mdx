Every filter is optional. Leave one blank and it doesn't narrow anything.

## Keyword

What the channels are about. This is what YouTube gets searched for.

## Subscribers min / max

Only channels inside this subscriber range. Handy for skipping giants and finding channels you could realistically catch.

## Avg views min / max

Only channels whose average views per video (over the last <Fact k="channels.avgViewsDays" unit="day" />) land in this range. Channels showing **Views pending** or no recent uploads can't be judged yet, so they stay in the results, listed after the ones that can.

## Upload frequency

How often the channel posts: **Any**, **Weekly**, `2-4 per week` or **Daily+**.

## Monetized

**Any**, **Yes** or **No**.

## Language and Country

Pick one or more. Only channels in those languages or countries come back.

## Channel age

Pick a date. Only channels created after it show up. Good for finding newer channels that grew fast.

## Reset

**Reset** clears every filter back to its default.
