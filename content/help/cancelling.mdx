1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Cancel plan**.
3. Confirm.

Cancelling takes effect at the end of your billing period, not straight away. Until then you keep everything you paid for. Your plan shows **Cancelling at period end**.

## Billing details

**Manage plan** opens the billing portal for your payment details.

Want your money back? See [Refunds](/help/refunds).
