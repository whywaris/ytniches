## Three views

- **List** is a sortable table. Best for scanning a lot of channels fast.
- **Grid** shows channels as cards.
- **Comparison** lines up channels side by side.

## The columns

- **Channel**: Name and avatar
- **Subs**: Subscriber count
- **Videos**: Public videos on the channel
- **Avg views**: Average views per video published in the last <Fact k="channels.avgViewsDays" unit="day" />. **Views pending** means we haven't collected the channel's videos yet (usually within a few hours). **No uploads in** that window means the channel hasn't posted recently.
- **Upload freq**: How often the channel posts
- **Monetized**: Whether the channel looks monetized
- **Language**: The channel's language
- **Country**: The channel's country

## Paging and sorting

Moving between pages and changing the sort order are free. They reuse your search's results for <Fact k="search.cacheHours" unit="hour" />. Change a filter or the keyword and it's a new search.
