## Create the workspace

Workspaces come with the **Team** plan. Once you're on it, go to [your workspace page](/workspace), enter a **Workspace name** and click **Create workspace**.

## Invite someone

Admins can invite. Open **Manage members**, enter the person's **Email**, pick a **Role**, and click **Send invite**.

They get an email with a link. The link works for <Fact k="invites.expiryDays" unit="day" />.

## Seats

A workspace has <Fact k="team.seats" unit="seat" />, and that includes you. Invites that are still open take a seat too. Workspace full? Remove a member to free a seat.

## What joining gets them

Members get Team features while they're in the workspace, even if they pay for a smaller plan or none at all:

- Their channels sync <Fact k="plan.team.sync" />.
- Their tracked channels count toward the workspace's shared pool. See [The shared channel pool](/help/shared-channel-pool).
- Email notifications and the digest.

Credits stay personal. Members spend their own, not the owner's.

If they leave, or the owner's Team plan ends, they go back to their own plan.
