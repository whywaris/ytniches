Open a saved prompt set and click **Regenerate**.

## Tell it what to change

Pick any of the quick fixes:

- **More casual**
- **Shorter**
- **Less clickbait**
- **More educational**
- **More detail**

Want something else? Write it in **Additional feedback**.

## Cost

Regenerating costs <Fact k="credits.promptRegenerate" unit="credit" />, less than a fresh generation.
