## In-app

Every plan gets in-app notifications. They show in the app and in your activity feed.

## Email

Email notifications are part of **Pro** and **Team**. The free trial includes them too, since it's Pro. Team workspace members get them as well.

Email is off until you turn it on.

## Choosing what you hear about

Go to **Settings → Notifications**. Under **By type**, each notification has an **In-app** and an **Email** switch:

- **New video**
- **View spike**
- **Cadence change**
- **Outlier detected**

## Per-channel overrides

Too much noise from one channel? Under **Per-channel overrides**, switch notifications off for just that channel.

## When your plan changes

If you move to a plan without email, emails stop. Your settings stay as they are. Move back to Pro or Team and emails start again.
