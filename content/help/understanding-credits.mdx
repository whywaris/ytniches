Credits pay for the actions that cost us real money: YouTube searches and AI generation. Everything else is part of your plan.

## What each action costs

- **Niche search**: <Fact k="credits.nicheSearch" unit="credit" />
- **Generate AI prompts**: <Fact k="credits.promptGenerate" unit="credit" />
- **Regenerate AI prompts**: <Fact k="credits.promptRegenerate" unit="credit" />
- **Thumbnail ideas**: <Fact k="credits.thumbnailIdeas" unit="credit" />
- **Another round of thumbnail ideas**: <Fact k="credits.thumbnailIdeasRegenerate" unit="credit" />
- **Tracking a channel**: Free

If an AI generation fails, the credits go straight back to your balance.

## Credits each month

- <Fact k="plan.starter.name" />: <Fact k="plan.starter.credits" unit="credit" />
- <Fact k="plan.pro.name" />: <Fact k="plan.pro.credits" unit="credit" />
- <Fact k="plan.team.name" />: <Fact k="plan.team.credits" unit="credit" />

The free trial comes with <Fact k="trial.credits" unit="credit" />, once. They don't refill during the trial.

Yearly plans get these credits every month, not once a year.

Credits are personal. On a Team workspace, each member spends their own.

## When credits expire

Your monthly credits last until your next billing month starts. Whatever you haven't used then expires, and the new month's credits arrive.

On **Team**, up to <Fact k="team.rollover" unit="credit" /> unused credits carry into the next month. They last one more month only, and you spend them first.

Bonus credits we give you never expire. You spend them last.

## Checking your balance

Go to **Settings → Billing**. **Credits remaining** shows your balance.

## Running out

Actions that cost credits stop working until you have enough again. Everything else keeps working. Need more? [Upgrading](/help/upgrading) covers moving to a bigger plan.
