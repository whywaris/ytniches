On a Team workspace, tracked channels come from one shared pool of <Fact k="team.pool" unit="channel" />.

Each member keeps their own list of tracked channels. The limit counts everyone's lists together. If one member tracks a lot, there's less room for the rest.

When the pool is full, nobody in the workspace can add a channel until someone removes one.

Leave the workspace and your channels count against your own plan's limit again.
