## During the beta

YTNiches costs <Fact k="beta.price" /> while we're in beta. Every account gets every <Fact k="trial.plan" /> feature with <Fact k="trial.credits" unit="credit" /> every month, and no card is needed. See [Free during beta](/help/free-trial).

## Plans after beta

These plans aren't available yet. We'll email every beta user <Fact k="beta.noticeDays" unit="day" /> before any paid plan starts.

## What else each plan includes

- **Starter**: everything in the table, with in-app notifications.
- **Pro**: adds email notifications, the email digest and quiet hours.
- **Team**: everything in Pro, plus a workspace with <Fact k="team.seats" unit="seat" />, tasks and a calendar. Tracked channels are shared across the workspace.
