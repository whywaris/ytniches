The digest is one email that rounds up what your tracked channels did. It's part of **Pro** and **Team**.

## What's in it

Up to <Fact k="digest.items" /> top outliers and up to <Fact k="digest.items" /> new videos from your tracked channels. A daily digest covers the last day. A weekly one covers the last week.

Nothing happened? Then no email. We don't send empty digests.

## Turning it on

Go to **Settings → Notifications**, under **Digest & quiet hours**:

- **Digest cadence**: **Off**, **Daily** or **Weekly**.
- **Digest day**: which day a weekly digest arrives.

## When it arrives

Around <Fact k="digest.time" />. There's no time zone setting yet, so that's <Fact k="digest.time" /> UTC for everyone.

## If your plan changes

We check your plan each time a digest is due. If your plan no longer includes it, the digest pauses. Your settings stay saved. Upgrade again and it picks back up.
