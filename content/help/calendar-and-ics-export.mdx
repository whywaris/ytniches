Open **Calendar** from the sidebar.

## Add an entry

Click **Add entry**, or click a date. Admins and Editors can add and edit entries. Viewers can see them.

## Export to your calendar

Click **Export .ics** to download the entries you're looking at as a file. Import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

The export is a one-off copy. It doesn't update itself. Changed the plan? Export again.
