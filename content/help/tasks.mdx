Open **Tasks** from the sidebar and click **New task**.

Each task has:

- **Title**
- **Description**
- **Assignee**: someone in the workspace
- **Due date**
- **Status**: **Open**, **In progress** or **Done**

Admins and Editors can create and edit tasks. Viewers can see them.
