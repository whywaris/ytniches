## Run the search

1. Open **Niche Finder** from the sidebar.
2. Type a **Keyword**, like "stoic philosophy" or "true crime".
3. Add filters if you want them. [Filters explained](/help/niche-finder-filters) covers each one.
4. Click **Search**.

A search costs <Fact k="credits.nicheSearch" unit="credit" />. You're only charged when the search succeeds.

Paging through the results or re-sorting them is free. So is running the exact same search again within <Fact k="search.cacheHours" unit="hour" />.

## Look at what came back

Results show as a **List** by default. Switch to **Grid** or **Comparison** with the view toggle. [Reading results](/help/reading-results) explains the columns.

## Keep the good ones

Found a channel you want to follow? Click **Save to tracking**. It moves into Competitor Tracking, where YTNiches keeps an eye on it for you. Saving is free.
