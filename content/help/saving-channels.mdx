Click **Save to tracking** on any result. The channel shows up in **Competitor Tracking**, and YTNiches starts syncing it.

Saving a channel is free. It doesn't use credits.

On a Team workspace, everyone's channels count toward one shared total. See [The shared channel pool](/help/shared-channel-pool).

Hit your limit? Remove a channel you've stopped caring about, or upgrade.

## Stop tracking

Click **Remove from tracking**. That frees up the slot straight away.
