## The channel was only just added

Outliers are found when a channel syncs. Give it until the next sync. See [How often channels sync](/help/sync-frequency).

## Not enough history

A baseline needs at least <Fact k="outliers.minPrior" unit="video" /> earlier videos. Before that, YTNiches can't tell what's normal for the channel, so it doesn't call anything an outlier.

## Nothing has broken out

Plenty of channels are steady. If no video has reached <Fact k="outliers.multiplier" /> the channel's baseline, there's no outlier to show. That's a finding too: the channel is consistent, not explosive.

## It's outside your date range

**Your outliers** shows videos published in the last month by default. Switch the filter to <Fact k="outliers.longerWindows" /> to see older ones. **Trending** also only covers outliers found in the last <Fact k="outliers.trendingDays" unit="day" />.
