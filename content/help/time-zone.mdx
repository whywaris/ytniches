Your time zone decides when your [email digest](/help/email-digest) arrives and when [quiet hours](/help/quiet-hours) apply.

## How we set it

The first time you open the app, YTNiches reads your time zone from your browser and saves it. After that, it doesn't change on its own, even if you travel.

## Changing it

Open the account menu (your avatar, top right) and choose **Profile**. Pick your **Time zone** and click **Save changes**. What you pick here always wins.
