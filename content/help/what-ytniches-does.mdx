Most niche tools stop once you've found a niche. YTNiches keeps going.

## Research

- **Niche Finder** searches YouTube channels by keyword, size, views, upload pace, country and language.
- **Competitor Tracking** follows the channels you pick and logs what they do: new videos, view spikes, changes in upload pace.
- **Your outliers** shows the videos from channels you track that beat their own channel's usual views by a wide margin, and Niche Finder's **Breakout videos** tab shows them across every channel we've discovered. Those are the ideas worth stealing.

## Create

- **AI Prompts** turns a video into title variants, hooks, thumbnail concepts, a script outline and a description template.
- **Thumbnail ideas** gives you thumbnail concepts for any outlier, straight from the outlier card.

## Plan

On the Team plan you get a shared **Workspace** with **Tasks** and a content **Calendar**, so a small team can ship together.
