## Admin

Everything an Editor can do, plus:

- Invite and remove members.
- Change members' roles.
- Delete the workspace.

## Editor

- Create and edit tasks.
- Add and edit calendar entries.

## Viewer

- See tasks and the calendar. Viewers can't change anything.

The person who creates the workspace is its owner, and starts as an Admin.

## Leaving

Anyone except the owner can leave with **Leave workspace**. There's no way to hand ownership to someone else yet. If you're the owner and want out, delete the workspace.
