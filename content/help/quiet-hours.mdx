Quiet hours stop notification emails during a window you choose. They're part of **Pro** and **Team**, like email itself.

## Setting them

Go to **Settings → Notifications** and set **Quiet hours start** and **Quiet hours end**.

A window can cross midnight. A start in the evening and an end in the morning means quiet all night.

## What they do

- Emails that would arrive during quiet hours aren't sent. They don't queue up for later.
- In-app notifications still arrive as normal.
- The digest isn't affected.

## Time zone

There's no time zone setting yet, so quiet hours run on UTC. Set your window in UTC for now.
