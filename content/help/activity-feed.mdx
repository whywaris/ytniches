The activity feed on **Competitor Tracking** lists what your tracked channels did since their last sync, newest first.

## New video

The channel published a video.

## View spike

One of the channel's videos crossed a view milestone. The milestones are <Fact k="tracking.milestones" /> views.

## Upload cadence changed

The channel's upload pace moved. YTNiches takes its average uploads per week over the last <Fact k="tracking.cadenceWindowWeeks" unit="week" />. If that average moves by at least <Fact k="tracking.cadenceThreshold" unit="upload" /> per week between syncs, it shows up here.

## Outliers

When one of the channel's videos becomes an outlier, it lands in the feed too. [What an outlier is](/help/what-is-an-outlier) explains the rule.

## How fresh is it?

As fresh as the last sync. See [How often channels sync](/help/sync-frequency).

## How long it's kept

Feed items stay for <Fact k="youtube.maxAgeDays" unit="day" />. YouTube only lets us keep its data that long, so older items are deleted.
