An outlier is a video that did far better than its own channel usually does. Those videos tell you what an audience wants right now.

## The rule

YTNiches takes the average views of the channel's previous <Fact k="outliers.baselineWindow" unit="video" />. That's the channel's baseline. A video that reaches <Fact k="outliers.multiplier" /> that baseline is an outlier.

Because the baseline is the channel's own, a small channel can have outliers too. A video with modest views can still be a huge hit for that channel.

## Where outliers come from

Only from channels you track. Track more channels in a niche and you'll see more of its outliers.

## The Outliers page

- **Feed** lists outliers as they're found, newest first.
- **Grid** ranks the top outliers over the date range you pick.
- **Trending** ranks outliers found in the last <Fact k="outliers.trendingDays" unit="day" />.

Rankings favour recent outliers. Each outlier stays on the page for <Fact k="youtube.maxAgeDays" unit="day" />, because YouTube only lets us keep its data that long.

## What to do with one

Every outlier card has **Extract prompts**, which opens AI Prompts for that video, and **Get thumbnail ideas**.
