## During the beta

Paid plans aren't available yet. Everyone uses YTNiches free while we're in beta, so there's nothing to upgrade to. See [Free during beta](/help/free-trial).

You can still see what each plan will include on [Plans](/help/plans).

## When paid plans start

We'll email you <Fact k="beta.noticeDays" unit="day" /> before paid plans start, with how to pick one. Questions before then? Email us at <SupportLink />.
