## From the free trial

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Upgrade**.
3. Pick a plan and whether to pay monthly or yearly.
4. Pay at checkout.

Your new plan starts once the payment goes through.

## Between paid plans

Already on a paid plan and want a different one? Email us at <SupportLink /> and we'll switch you over. We do this by hand for now so you're never billed for two plans at once.
