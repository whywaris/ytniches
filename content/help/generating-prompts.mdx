Open **AI Prompts**. Under **Generate prompts from a video**, pick your source.

## Pick a video

- **From tracked channel**: choose one of your tracked channels, then one of its most-viewed videos.
- **From URL**: paste the link to any YouTube video.

## Point it at your audience

- **Target audience**: who you're making the video for, like "beginners" or "US teens". Optional.
- **Tone**: **Neutral**, **Casual**, **Educational**, **Dramatic** or **Clickbait-lite**.

## What you get

- **Title variants**
- **Hook variants**
- **Thumbnail concepts**
- **Script outline**
- **Description template**

YTNiches works from the video's title, description and tags. It doesn't read the video itself or its captions.

## Cost

Generating costs <Fact k="credits.promptGenerate" unit="credit" />. If generation fails, you get the credits back.

Every set you generate is saved in AI Prompts, so you can come back to it. Your prompts stay for good. The source video's YouTube details don't: we clear them after <Fact k="youtube.maxAgeDays" unit="day" /> without a refresh, so an old prompt may show **Video details expired**. Regenerating it fetches them again.
