## Money-back guarantee

Your first purchase comes with a <Fact k="refunds.guaranteeDays" />-day money-back guarantee. That covers monthly and yearly plans.

## After that

- **Monthly plans**: no refunds after the first <Fact k="refunds.guaranteeDays" unit="day" />.
- **Yearly plans**: cancel within the first <Fact k="refunds.annualWindowDays" unit="day" /> and we refund the unused whole months.

## Exceptions

We look at these case by case:

- A billing error, like a double charge or the wrong plan.
- An extended service outage.
- A documented account compromise.

## How to ask

Email <SupportLink /> from the address on your account and tell us what happened.
