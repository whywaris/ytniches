You can sign in two ways: **Continue with Google**, or an **email address and password**. Both lead to the same account if they use the same email.

## Signing up with email

1. On the sign-up page, enter your email and a password.
2. Your password needs at least <Fact k="auth.passwordMinLength" unit="character" />, with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter and a number. We also reject passwords that have appeared in known data breaches.
3. We email you a verification link. Click it to start using YTNiches. The link works once, for <Fact k="auth.linkExpiryHours" unit="hour" />.

You can't use the account, and your free plan doesn't start, until the email is verified. Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder, or use **Resend the link** on the "Check your email" page. You can resend up to <Fact k="auth.emailsPerHour" unit="time" /> an hour.

## Forgot your password

1. On the log-in page, click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email. If there's an account, we send a link to set a new password. It works once, for <Fact k="auth.linkExpiryHours" unit="hour" />.
3. Choose a new password. Saving it signs you out on your other devices.

Signed up with Google? Resetting your password adds one to the same account, so you can use either way after that.

## Too many attempts

After <Fact k="auth.lockoutFailures" unit="wrong password" /> in <Fact k="auth.lockoutMinutes" unit="minute" />, password sign-in for that email pauses for <Fact k="auth.lockoutMinutes" unit="minute" />, and we email the account owner. **Continue with Google** still works during the pause.
